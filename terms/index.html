2. What the app does

Poppd lets you record movies you have watched, rate them on a five-point scale, build a taste profile, find people with similar taste, and get recommendations.

Recommendations are suggestions, not promises. A match score is a compatibility score based on your ratings and other users' — not a prediction that you will enjoy a film. Movie information, running times, age certifications, posters and artwork come from a third party (TMDB) and may be incomplete, out of date or wrong; do not rely on a certification shown in the app as an authoritative age rating.
