2. What the app does

Poppd lets you record movies you have watched, rate them on a four-point scale, build a taste profile, find people with similar taste, and get recommendations.

Recommendations are suggestions, not promises. A match score is a compatibility score based on your ratings and other users' — not a prediction that you will enjoy a film. Movie information, running times, age certifications, posters and artwork come from a third party (TMDB) and may be incomplete, out of date or wrong; do not rely on a certification shown in the app as an authoritative age rating.

3. Your account

  • You need an account. Today that means an email address and a password. (Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google are planned but not yet available.)
  • You must be at least 13 to use Poppd. We ask you to confirm that when you create your profile and we record the confirmation, but we do not verify it and we do not collect your date of birth.
  • One account per person. Creating accounts to inflate your own influence on recommendations is not allowed (§5).
  • Keep your sign-in details to yourself. Tell us at privacy@poppd.io if you think someone else has access.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is deleted and what is kept.

4. Your username, photo and bio

You choose a username and may add a photo and a short bio. They must not impersonate anyone, and must not contain slurs or abusive terms — the app blocks a list of these outright, and a blocked username or bio will simply be refused.

What we do with them — under legal review. They stay yours. We need your permission to store them and to show them inside the app to the people your privacy settings allow — that is what a profile is — and to process your ratings to produce recommendations and taste matches for you and for other users, as the Privacy Policy describes. We do not use them anywhere else: not in advertising, not to train anything, and we do not sell them. The formal licence wording is being finalised with legal advisers before public release.

We may remove or require a change to a username, photo or bio that breaks these terms.

5. Fair use

Do not:

  • rate films in bulk, or in patterns designed to push a film up or down other people's recommendations — the recommendation engine is a shared resource and manipulating it affects everyone;
  • create fake or automated accounts, or run scripts against the app or its API;
  • harass, impersonate or abuse other users;
  • submit reports you know to be false, or report the same account repeatedly to harass them — reporting is rate-limited and repeated reports of the same account will be refused;
  • attempt to access data the app does not show you, or to work around the privacy settings of another account;
  • copy, scrape or redistribute movie data or artwork from the app.

Being straight with you about the beta: there is no admin console for suspensions and no appeals workflow — anything of that kind would be done by hand, by one person, and we are not going to promise a response time or a process we have not built. The formal wording of our suspension and termination rights, and of any appeal route, is being finalised with legal advisers before public release.
